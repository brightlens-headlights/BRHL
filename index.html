FAQ

Questions?

A few quick answers before you book. For anything specific to your vehicle, contact BrightLens.

Where does BrightLens serve?

BrightLens serves customers in Lincoln, Nebraska.

Can every cloudy headlight be restored?

Not always. Exterior surface haze and oxidation can often be improved, while cracks, moisture inside the assembly, severe damage or internal component problems may require repair or replacement.

How do I book?

Use the Google Calendar appointment button above, call (402) 413-8694, or send a message.

How long does an appointment take?

Timing can vary depending on the vehicle and condition of the lenses. Contact BrightLens for an estimate for your specific headlights.
